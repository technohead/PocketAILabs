Engineered on-device

A cartoon machine that runs in your pocket

Kartoonify is built to turn high-quality AI art into something fast and private enough to use anywhere. No server, no wait, no data ever leaving your device.

Private by design

Your photos stay yours.

Kartoonify was built around one rule: inference happens on the device. Your original photos never leave your phone, aren't sent to a server, and aren't used to train anything. Face data is held only in memory during processing and written to no database. We could not access your images even if we wanted to.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it free to use?

Kartoonify is free to download with a set of starter styles. Unlock every preset and unlimited generations with a one-time purchase.

Do I need a strong phone or internet connection?

Nope. The AI runs entirely on your device via its fastest engine (Metal, NPU, or GPU) and automatically falls back to the CPU on older phones. It works fully offline — on first launch it downloads the ~1 GB model once, then everything runs locally.

Where do my photos go?

Nowhere. All processing happens on your phone — no uploads, no cloud storage. Generated avatars live only in your camera roll, and any analytics are limited to non-image usage data.

Is it available for both iPhone and Android?

Yes. Kartoonify is built natively for iOS and Android, so you get the same private, on-device experience on either platform.
