How it works

From portrait to cartoon in one tap

  1. 01

    Take or choose a photo

    Point the camera at yourself, or pick a selfie from your gallery. Kartoonify locks onto your face instantly.

  2. 02

    Pick your style

    Browse the style presets — Cartoon, Anime, Oil Paint, Sketch and more — or write your own prompt.

  3. 03

    Stable Diffusion re-renders you

    On-device face detection protects your identity, then SD img2img re-renders you in your chosen style — all without ever leaving the phone.

  4. 04

    Refine, regenerate, enhance

    Drag the sliders to fine-tune the look, or hit Regenerate for a fresh take on the same style — then optionally sharpen faces (GFPGAN) and upscale (Real-ESRGAN). Save to your gallery, set as wallpaper, or share straight to social.

Engineered on-device

A cartoon machine tuned to every chip

Kartoonify talks to the fastest engine on the device, and every chip gets its own optimized pipeline. iOS runs straight into Apple’s Neural Engine; Snapdragon drives its NPU directly. No server, no wait, no data ever leaving your device.

  • 🍎iOS → Neural Engine— runs on Apple’s Core ML pipeline on the Neural Engine, with Metal as a lightweight fallback. This is the fast path — a generation is roughly 10–20× quicker than the older Metal-only route, using only a handful of sampling steps.
  • ⚙️Snapdragon → NPU— routes to Qualcomm’s QNN / HTP NPU, matched to the exact Snapdragon generation (V68, V69, V73+). Everything else on Android falls back to the Vulkan GPU, then the CPU.
  • 🔒100% private— your photos stay on your device and aren't uploaded or stored anywhere — unless you choose to share an image or turn it into a product.
  • 📡Works offline— generate avatars on a flight, in a tunnel, or anywhere without signal.
  • 🎯Face-perfect— two-pass mask-based preservation with ML Kit keeps your likeness accurate.
  • 🔄Graceful fallback— if a fast backend is unavailable (older phones, throttling), Kartoonify seamlessly moves to the next engine — never stalls.

Private by design

FAQ

Questions, answered

How much does it cost?

Kartoonify Lite is free to download — supported by a single banner ad. The full Kartoonify is a one-time purchase, no subscription: every style preset, custom prompts, unlimited generations, and no ads. Lite launches first; the full app follows close behind.

What’s the difference between Kartoonify Lite and Kartoonify?

Same private, on-device engine in both. Lite is free (with one small banner ad) and ships with a set of starter styles — and it launches first. The full Kartoonify is a one-time purchase that adds every style preset, custom prompts, photo-realistic regeneration, unlimited generations, and an ad-free experience. Compare the editions.

Can I re-roll a result I didn't love?

Absolutely. Hit Regenerate and Kartoonify re-renders with a fresh random seed using your current style and settings — infinite variations, no limit. You can also fine-tune with the detail sliders before deciding.

Do I need a strong phone or internet connection?

Nope. Kartoonify uses the phone’s fastest engine automatically — Apple’s Neural Engine on iOS, Qualcomm’s NPU on Snapdragon, the Vulkan GPU otherwise — and falls back to the CPU on older phones, so it runs smoothly almost anywhere. It works fully offline — on first launch it downloads the ~1 GB model once, then everything runs locally.

Where do my photos go?

On your phone. All processing happens locally — nothing is uploaded or stored in the cloud unless you choose to do so. Generated avatars live only in your camera roll. The only time an image leaves your device is if you actively share it (email, instant message, SMS, or another app) or turn it into a product through our print partners — and even then only the finished artwork you choose to send, never your originals. Any analytics are limited to non-image usage data.

How does the "Make It Real" shop work?

After generating a cartoon you can turn it into a physical product — a canvas, poster, phone case, or mug — straight from the app. Orders are fulfilled by our print partners; it's a one-off purchase with no impact on your generated files.

Is it available for both iPhone and Android?

Yes. Kartoonify is built natively for iOS and Android, so you get the same private, on-device experience on either platform.
