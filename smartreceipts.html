Features

Everything you need to keep receipts under control

Built to run natively and offline, on the fastest chip your phone offers.

📸

Camera & gallery scan

Point your camera at a receipt, or pick one from your gallery. On-device AI extracts the store, date, items, total, and currency — no uploads.

🧠

Local AI extraction

A multimodal on-device model reads the receipt text. Runs in-process with an NPU/GPU-first pipeline and an automatic CPU fallback.

📊

Insights & analytics

See spending by category, track trends, and get monthly projections — all organized from your receipts, never leaving your phone.

💳

Multi-currency

Per-receipt currency detection (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY) with locale-aware formatting and a configurable default currency.

🔐

Biometric lock

Lock the app behind Face ID or fingerprint, with configurable auto-lock timing. Your receipts stay yours.

📦

Backup & restore

Export everything to portable .srcpt files and restore instantly. Automatic backups keep your history safe, locally.
