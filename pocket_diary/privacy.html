Privacy Policy for Pocket Diary

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pocket AI Labs ("Pocket AI Labs", "we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the Pocket Diary mobile application for iOS and Android (the "App").

This Policy should be read together with our Terms of Service.

1. The Short Version

  • Your diary never leaves your device. Entries, chat messages with the Nova companion, photos, voice memos, and drawings are stored only on your device — inside the app's sandboxed storage — and are never transmitted to us or anyone else. All AI features (the Nova companion, voice dictation, safety detection) run entirely on your device. We could not read your diary even if we wanted to.
  • We collect basic analytics and crash diagnostics. These tell us how the App is used and when it breaks. They contain no diary text, no chat content, no passcodes, and no contact data — only counts, event names, and device metadata.
  • We do not require an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information, and the App contains no advertising and no third-party ad trackers.
  • The only network connections are for analytics/crash reports, AI model downloads, the optional premium purchase handled by your app store, and actions you take yourself (such as calling or texting a helpline or trusted contact).

2. Information We Do Not Collect

This is the core design principle of the App. Pocket AI Labs does not collect, transmit, receive, or store:

  • Your diary entries — text, mood, photos, drawings, or voice memos;
  • Your conversations with the Nova companion, or any content you type, dictate, or attach to it;
  • Your passcode, PIN, or biometric data;
  • Your trusted contact's details, or the contents of your address book;
  • Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address;
  • Your precise (GPS) location;
  • An index of your photo library, files, or entries.

There is no server-side diary, no cloud sync, and no account. Everything you write or record exists only on your device, protected by your chosen lock (see Section 10).

The only ways content can leave your device are actions you initiate yourself — for example, attaching a photo, tapping a helpline link, or calling or messaging a trusted contact — and even then the content goes to the recipient or service you chose, never to us.

On-device AI processing

The Nova companion, voice dictation, and the safety classifier all run as AI models on your device. Nothing you write or say is sent to a cloud AI service. The App's Dart code contains no general-purpose HTTP networking at all — this is enforced by an automated check in our codebase.

4. Information We Do Collect (Analytics and Diagnostics)

The App collects a limited amount of usage and reliability data via Google Firebase. This is the only data that leaves your device automatically.

4.1 Usage Analytics

Collected via Firebase Analytics:

  • App lifecycle events — for example, launch, foreground/background transitions, onboarding completion, and lock/unlock events (method and success only — never the passcode);
  • Feature usage counts — for example, an entry_saved event carrying attachment counts (photo_count, voice_count, has_text, has_mood), persona selections, model download/init events, theme selections, and premium purchase-funnel events;
  • Safety threshold events — an event may record that a support card was shown (e.g. entry_support_shown, nova_crisis_shown). It never records what was written;
  • Automatically collected Firebase Analytics data — app instance identifier, app version, device model, operating system version, language/locale, country derived coarsely from IP address, session count, and session duration. IP addresses are used by Firebase for coarse geographic inference and are not retained by us.

4.2 Crash and Error Diagnostics

Collected via Firebase Crashlytics:

  • Crashes and unhandled errors on iOS and Android, including error messages and stack traces;
  • Device state at time of crash — device model, OS version, app version, and memory state.

4.3 What Analytics Data Never Contains

Analytics and crash reports never include diary text, chat messages, voice recordings, photos, drawings, passcodes, contact names or numbers, or the content of any file. Event parameters carry names, counts, and enum values only — this constraint is part of the App's telemetry design, not just this policy.

4.4 Why We Collect It

  • To measure reliability and fix crashes and defects;
  • To understand which features are used and how the App performs across device classes;
  • To prioritise improvements and detect regressions between releases.

We do not use this data for advertising, profiling, or resale.

5. Network Activity

The App communicates over the network only for:

  1. Analytics and crash reporting to Google Firebase, as described in Section 4;
  2. AI model downloads, when a required model (Nova language model or dictation model) is not already present on your device. These downloads fetch model files only — no diary content or personal data is sent as part of a model download;
  3. The optional premium unlock purchase, handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies (see Section 7);
  4. Actions you initiate yourself — tapping a crisis helpline link (for example, findahelpline.com or a national hotline), or calling or texting your trusted contact. These open in your browser or phone app under your control;
  5. Local notifications are not network activity — optional parent/guardian alerts are delivered as on-device notifications only.

The App contains no advertising and contacts no ad networks or third-party trackers.

8. Data Retention

  • Your diary content: retained only on your device, for as long as you keep it. We hold no copies and cannot delete, recover, or restore it. Uninstalling the App deletes it.
  • Your passcode: only a salted hash exists, in your device's secure storage. We cannot recover a forgotten passcode — by design, there is no reset mechanism through us.
  • Analytics data: retained by Firebase Analytics according to our configured retention setting (default: 14 months), after which it is deleted or aggregated.
  • Crash reports: retained by Crashlytics for approximately 90 days for open issues, per Google's standard retention.

9. Your Rights and Choices

9.1 Limiting Analytics Collection

Analytics and crash diagnostics as described in Section 4 are collected as part of the App's normal operation. The App does not currently provide an in-app setting to disable them. You can limit or stop collection by:

  • iOS: reviewing the controls under Settings → Privacy & Security, including Analytics & Improvements;
  • Android: reviewing the controls under Settings → Google → Ads and the App's entry in Settings → Apps;
  • Uninstalling the App, which stops all collection.

9.2 Controlling Your On-Device Data

  • Entries can be deleted individually in the App; uninstalling removes everything.
  • Permissions (camera, photos, microphone, biometric, notifications, contacts) can be revoked at any time in device settings.
  • The premium entitlement can be restored on a new device via the store's purchase-restore mechanism.

9.3 Legal Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability. Because your diary content never reaches us and we maintain no accounts, the data we can act on is limited to analytics and crash records associated with your app instance identifier. To make a request, contact us at the address in Section 14; we may ask for information to locate the relevant records.

EEA/UK users: we rely on our legitimate interests in maintaining a reliable, secure, and functional application as the legal basis for collecting the analytics and crash diagnostics described in Section 4. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests, and you may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

California users: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA, and we do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information off-device. You have the right to know, delete, correct, and to be free from discrimination for exercising these rights.

10. Security

Your diary is protected by design, not just by policy:

  • Sandboxed storage. Diary entries and attachments are stored inside the app's private, sandboxed storage, accessible only to the App — other apps cannot read them. At-rest encryption (AES-256-GCM with a device-bound key) is built into the App behind a build flag and may be enabled in a future release; this policy will be updated if it ships.
  • Passcode hashing. Passcodes are stored only as salted SHA-256 hashes in flutter_secure_storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). The plaintext code is never stored or transmitted.
  • Biometrics stay with the OS. Face ID / fingerprint checks are performed entirely by your operating system through local_auth; the App never accesses biometric templates.
  • Screen protection. The App hides its contents in the app switcher and blocks screenshots/screen-recording where the platform allows it, and can require re-unlocking when backgrounded.
  • No network surface for content. The App's own code contains no general-purpose HTTP client — the only outbound connections are Firebase telemetry, model downloads, store purchases, and links you open yourself.
  • In transit. The limited analytics and crash data we collect is encrypted in transit (TLS) and stored by Google Firebase under its security controls.

The primary safeguard for your diary is your device itself — we strongly recommend a device passcode, biometric lock, and device encryption. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is designed to be private-by-default for everyone, including younger users who keep a diary — because nothing they write leaves the device, there is no diary content for us to collect from anyone. The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction) without a parent or guardian's involvement, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Optional parent/guardian features (a parent PIN panel and local alert notifications) exist so a guardian can be involved in a child's use. These operate entirely on-device: the alert is a local notification, and the guardian's involvement stays between the child and the guardian. Safety features may also surface helpline links; tapping them is always the user's own action.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us (limited in practice to analytics records), contact us and we will delete the associated records.

12. International Transfers

Analytics and crash data are processed by Google on infrastructure that may be located in the United States and other countries. Where personal data is transferred out of the EEA or UK, Google's Standard Contractual Clauses and supplementary measures apply. No diary content is transferred to us or to our analytics providers, because no diary content is collected by us.
